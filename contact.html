✦ Quick Answers ✦

Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know before your visit.

Do I need a reservation? +

Walk-ins are always welcome, but we strongly recommend reservations for dinner service and weekends, as we often fill up quickly. You can reserve directly through our website or by calling us.

Do you cater to dietary requirements? +

Absolutely. We offer vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options across our menu. Please mention any allergies or dietary restrictions when booking or speak to your server — our kitchen team will be happy to accommodate.

Can I host a private event at Lumina? +

Yes! We host intimate private events, corporate dinners, anniversary celebrations, and birthday gatherings. Our team will craft a bespoke menu and atmosphere tailored to your occasion. Contact us via the form above to get started.

Is there parking nearby? +

There is street parking available on Cinematic Ave and a public car park one block away on Flavor Street. We are also well-served by public transport — the nearest stop is Cinematic Square, a 2-minute walk.

Do you offer gift cards? +

Yes, Lumina gift cards are available in any denomination and make the perfect present. Pick one up in-store or email us at reserve@luminacafe.com and we'll arrange delivery.

What is your cancellation policy? +

We kindly ask for at least 24 hours' notice for cancellations or changes to your reservation. This helps us ensure other guests have the opportunity to secure a table. Late cancellations may incur a small fee for large groups.
